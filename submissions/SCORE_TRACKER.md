## Lessons learned

- **1-2pp local val lift = dead zone.** Two candidates with small local lifts both lost on Kaggle (0.29887, 0.29868). Don't submit unless the signal is clearly different from baseline.
- **The winner was a tiny perturbation** — 52 law cites added, 7 removed, 29/40 queries touched. Big structural changes have underperformed.
- **Court-dense additions hurt.** The winning config used `max_add_court=0` (law-only). Court FAISS recall ceiling is 64% but the trained classifier overfits to baseline patterns.
- **Next improvement needs structurally different signal**, not another perturbation in the same neighborhood.
- **Public/private split matters more now.** The 0.32107 submission won public LB with targeted procedural deltas, but public uses only ~50% of test. Preserve 0.30911 and 0.30257 as final-submission hedges.
- **Final submissions limit is 2**, not 3. Pick one aggressive public winner and one conservative hedge; do not select two near-duplicates.
- **Tiny additive lifts above 0.32107 are not enough.** `mined_wide_positive_safe` improved val by +0.45pp with only four test adds, but public LB fell to 0.31993.
- **2026-04-24 UTC quota burn did not find a jump.** `cached_llm_signal_gemini_law1` scored 0.31743, `private_safe_selector_aggressive` scored 0.30821, and `bf2_top1_opus_high_safe` scored 0.28650. Current best remains 0.32107.
- **Even strong local post-32107 candidates are not lifting public.** `highupside_article_phd_atsg` scored 0.31901 and `adversarial_error_analysis` scored 0.31988 despite the latter having val 0.4318, LB90 0.3848, balanced +5/-5 churn, and diverse STRONG PROMOTE. Current best remains 0.32107.
- **The overnight fullpower vote family is not a public breakthrough.** `fullpower_s3_vote_top1`, `fullpower_s3_vote_top2`, and `fullpower_s2_vote_top31` all had very high local F1/LB90 but scored only 0.31779-0.31791 on public. Do not spend more submissions on near-neighbor fullpower vote variants without a new independent signal.
